Rollie Fingers
Last published: 1st

Rollie emerges from the All-Star break alone in first at 105 points, seven and a half clear of the two teams sharing second. The hunter has become the hunted, and I'm pissed. Wilyer Abreu hit five homers with a 1.006 OPS, Elly De La Cruz added three homers and a steal, and the roster still owns all twelve points in HR and OPS.
