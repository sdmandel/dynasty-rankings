Seam Heads dominating as usual, Rollie Fingers still chasing the top spot, etc., etc. The real action happened in the middle: Vin Mazzaro fan club's trade binge with Trazadone finally bought them two spots, and Jonesin' fell three for reasons the transaction log does not explain.
